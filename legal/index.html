Privacy notice

What this website collects

The site has no accounts, forms, or advertising, and it does not follow you across other websites. Its fonts and map data are served from this website itself.

Visit counts

To see how many people read the site and which pages they visit, I use GoatCounter, a privacy-friendly analytics service. It uses no cookies and builds no profile of you. For each page view it records the page, the site that linked you here, your browser and screen size, and your approximate country. It does not store your IP address, and I only see totals, not individual visitors. The small counting script loads from GoatCounter's servers.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. To deliver and secure the site, GitHub processes technical information such as your IP address in its server logs. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

When you email me

I receive your name, email address, and whatever you write. I use this only to reply to you and to follow up on your request. My email is hosted by Microsoft (Outlook/Hotmail).

When you book a consultation

Bookings are handled by Cal.com (scheduling) and payments by PayPal. Cal.com collects your name, email address, the time you choose, and any answers you give to booking questions. PayPal processes your payment; I never see your card or bank details. I receive your name, email address, booking details, and the amount paid. Each service also handles your information under its own privacy policy.

How I use your information

  • To reply to messages and arrange and deliver consultations.

I do not sell your information, share it for marketing, or add you to mailing lists.

I keep emails and booking records for one year after our last contact, then delete them. Payment and invoice records are kept longer where Canadian tax law requires it (generally six years), and only for that purpose.

This website does not set cookies, and the visit counter described above does not use them either.
