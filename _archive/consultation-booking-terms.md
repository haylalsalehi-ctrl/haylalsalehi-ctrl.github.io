### Full consultation terms (booking version)

```html
    <h2 id="terms">Consultation terms</h2>
    <p>These terms apply when you book a paid consultation. By booking, you agree to them. The terms in effect when you book apply to that booking.</p>

    <h3>The service</h3>
    <p>One-on-one consultations by video call on Yemen and the wider Middle East and North Africa, in English or Arabic. Consultations provide analysis and opinion based on my research and on publicly available information.</p>

    <h3>Booking and payment</h3>
    <p>You book and pay in advance through Cal.com and PayPal, at the price shown when you book.</p>

    <h3>Cancellations, rescheduling, and refunds</h3>
    <ul>
      <li>If you cancel or reschedule at least <strong>24 hours</strong> before the start time, you can choose a full refund or a new time at no cost.</li>
      <li>Cancellations made less than 24 hours before the start time, and missed sessions, are not refunded.</li>
      <li>If I cancel or cannot attend, you get a full refund or a new time of your choice.</li>
    </ul>

    <h3>Not professional advice</h3>
    <p>Consultations are for information and analysis only. They are not legal, financial, investment, security, or safety advice, and I do not guarantee any outcome. Decisions you make remain your own. For travel or safety decisions, rely on official travel advisories and qualified security professionals.</p>

    <h3>Confidentiality</h3>
    <p>I keep what you share in a consultation confidential and will not disclose it without your permission, unless the law requires it. Please do not share information you are not authorized to share. In turn, I do not disclose confidential information belonging to my current or former employers or to anyone else.</p>

    <h3>Recording</h3>
    <p>Sessions are not recorded unless we both agree in advance.</p>

    <h3>Lawful use and sanctions</h3>
    <p>I do not provide services to any person or entity subject to sanctions under Canadian, United States, or United Nations law, to anyone acting on their behalf, or for any unlawful purpose. I may decline or end an engagement for these or other reasonable grounds; if I end one, I will refund any unused time.</p>

    <h3>Independence</h3>
    <p>I provide consultations in my personal capacity, not on behalf of Western University or any current or former employer.</p>

    <h3>Liability</h3>
    <p>To the extent permitted by law, my total liability for any consultation is limited to the fee you paid for it, and I am not liable for indirect or consequential losses.</p>

    <h3>Governing law</h3>
    <p>These terms are governed by the laws of the Province of Ontario and the federal laws of Canada that apply there.</p>
```
